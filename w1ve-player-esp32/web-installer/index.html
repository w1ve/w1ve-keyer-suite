W1VE ESP32 Player

Flash the Player firmware to an ESP32-S3 board directly from your browser.

Your browser can't talk to USB serial devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer.

DevKit-C and other 16 MB modules.

How to flash

  1. Connect the ESP32-S3 to this computer with a USB data cable.
  2. Pick the card that matches your board's flash size, then click Install.
  3. Choose the board's serial port in the browser prompt. If no port appears, hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, then try again.
  4. Let the flash finish. The board reboots into the setup access point W1VE-Player-XXXX.

Not sure of your flash size? A fresh install erases the chip; picking a layout larger than the physical flash will fail safely at flash time, so start with the smaller option if unsure.
